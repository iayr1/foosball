Sling Arena · Android

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026 App ID online.athlorun.sling Publisher Sling Arena

In short: Sling Arena does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. The game works fully offline, and your progress stays on your own device.

Information we collect

None. Sling Arena does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. There are no user accounts and no sign-in.

Information stored on your device

To remember your game, Sling Arena saves the following only in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet:

  • Game progress: level, experience points, coins, gems, chests and tournament round.
  • Items you have unlocked and equipped, such as puck skins, boards and effects.
  • Match statistics and achievements, such as wins, streaks and best times.
  • Settings: sound effects, vibration and the aim guide.

This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Internet, ads and analytics

The released app does not request internet access. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party services that receive information about you.

Permissions

Sling Arena does not request any special Android permissions. Vibration feedback uses the standard system haptics, which you can turn off at any time in the game's Settings.

In-app currency and purchases

Coins and gems are earned by playing and are stored only on your device. The game has no real-money purchases.

Children

Sling Arena is suitable for players of all ages. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Google Play

When you download the app, Google Play may collect information under Google's own privacy policy. That data is handled by Google, not by Sling Arena.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding online features, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before that version is released.
