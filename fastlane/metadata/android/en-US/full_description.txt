Sling Arena is a fast, glossy tabletop flicking game. Pull a puck back like a slingshot, let it fly through the gap in the wall, and be the first to clear every puck from your side of the board.

EASY TO PLAY, HARD TO MASTER
• Drag a puck back, aim, and release. Pull further for more power.
• Only the gap in the centre wall lets pucks through, so line up your shots.
• Bounce off the rails for bank shots and knock two pucks through at once for combos.

SIX WAYS TO PLAY
• Vs AI: four bots, from friendly Pip to the sharp-shooting Legend, Nova.
• Pass & Play: challenge a friend face to face on one phone.
• Tournament: win three rounds in a row to take the cup.
• Time Attack: clear your side before the clock runs out.
• Daily Challenge: a fresh challenge with bonus rewards every day.
• Academy: four short lessons that teach aiming, bank shots and combos.

EARN, UNLOCK, CUSTOMISE
• Collect coins, gems and chests every time you win.
• Style your arena with puck skins, board designs, glowing bands and trail effects.
• Unlock 12 achievements and climb from Bronze to Legend rank.
• Track your wins, streaks, best combos and fastest victories.

MADE TO FEEL GREAT
• Glossy, colourful visuals with smooth physics.
• Satisfying sounds and vibration feedback you can switch off.
• Works in portrait and landscape, on phones and tablets.
• Plays fully offline: no account, no ads, no data collection.

Choose 3, 5 or 7 pucks per side, pick your opponent, and start flicking!
